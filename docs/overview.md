# MyUNO Capital — Product Overview & Vision

> **One-sentence pitch:** MyUNO Capital is an AI operating system that gives solo entrepreneurs a full-stack virtual team — strategy, product, engineering, marketing, sales, support, finance, and exit planning — so one person can run multiple businesses end-to-end, from first idea to successful exit.

**Tagline:** *The full virtual team for solo entrepreneurs: validate ideas, build, grow, and exit your business from one AI-powered platform.*

**Category:** Autonomous AI Operating System / Solo Founder Platform

---

## Core Mission

- Make it possible for **one person** to operate at the level of a full founding team and staff.
- Cover the **entire business lifecycle**: idea discovery and validation, product design and development, go-to-market and growth, operations and support, funding and exit (acquisition / M&A / orderly wind-down).
- Be accessible and useful to **tens of thousands of solo entrepreneurs**, with affordable pricing, simple onboarding, and a secure, scalable multi-tenant architecture.

---

## The Problem: Solo Founder Constraints

Solo founders — especially location-independent founders in hubs like Phuket, Bali, and Chiang Mai — face unique constraints that throttle their ability to ship and grow:

- **No real team.** One person must handle strategy, product, engineering, marketing, sales, customer service, finance, legal, and admin. Constant context switching slows progress and leads to burnout.
- **Limited capital.** Hiring a full-stack developer, marketer, designer, and ops person isn't realistic early on. Agencies and consultants are expensive and still require heavy founder involvement.
- **No structured path from idea to exit.** There are many tools for isolated tasks (email marketing, project management), but very few systems guide a founder from *raw idea → validation → MVP → scale → exit*.
- **Isolation and decision risk.** No co-founder to challenge assumptions. It's hard to know when an idea is worth pursuing, when to pivot, and when to prepare for exit.
- **Remote lifestyle constraints.** Many solo founders operate across time zones and want businesses that run 24/7 without their constant presence.

---

## The Opportunity

Modern LLMs and AI agents can now handle complex reasoning and multi-step workflows, work across product, code, marketing, sales, and support, and operate continuously with human oversight at key checkpoints. This unlocks:

- **Solo empires** — one person owning multiple cash-flowing businesses.
- **High-leverage side projects** that can be incubated into full businesses.
- A path to **repeatable exits** for solo founders, not only VC-backed startups.

---

## The Solution: End-to-End AI "Company OS"

MyUNO Capital is an **Autonomous AI Operating System** that:

- Learns your goals, constraints, risk appetite, and working style.
- Composes a **virtual team of agents** (strategy, product, dev, growth, sales, support, finance, legal-lite, exit).
- Runs your business as a set of **end-to-end workflows**: idea → validation → MVP → launch → scale → optimize → exit.
- Gives you a **single console** to set goals and limits, approve important actions, see metrics and progress, and pause, adjust, or override at any time.

A central [Decision Hub](./decision-hub.md) lets you compare options and make decisions with data — not gut feeling — using Risk, Complexity, and Potential scores plus an [elite advisor layer](./roadmap.md).

## How It Works in 60 Seconds

Imagine you're a solo founder in Phuket with a rough idea for a B2B SaaS micro-product.

1. **You set a goal.** You create a new business in the Founder Console and type your objective in plain language: *"Profitably reach $10k MRR in 12 months."* The **StrategyAgent** turns it into OKRs, a roadmap, and a first-week action plan.
2. **You validate the idea — cheaply.** The **IdeaValidationAgent** researches market size and competition, designs validation experiments (landing page, ads, cold outreach, pre-orders), and produces **Risk, Complexity, and Potential scores (0–100)** plus a validation report. Results land in the **Decision Hub**, where the elite advisors weigh in. You pick the strongest option; the decision and its reasoning are logged.
3. **You build the MVP.** The **ProductManagerAgent** writes specs, personas, and user stories. The **DevAgent** writes and refactors code in your repo with proper branching and PRs, integrating payments (Stripe), auth, and analytics. High-risk actions like production deploys pause for your approval.
4. **You get your first customers.** The **MarketingStrategyAgent** picks acquisition channels and plans campaigns; the **AdsAgent** launches and optimizes ad campaigns within strict budget guardrails (e.g., *"Ask before spending > $100/day"*). The **ContentAgent** writes the landing page and email sequences in your brand voice.
5. **You stay in control.** Dashboards show MRR, churn, CAC, and LTV in real time. Every significant agent and human action is captured in the **Global Activity Log** — fuel for debugging, trust, and eventual exit due diligence.

One person. A full team's output. From first idea to first customers — and, when the time comes, to a clean, well-documented exit.
